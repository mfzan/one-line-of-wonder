One line of text written to make a reader feel wonder, built for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s "One Line of Wonder" bounty. It follows the shape the judge's brief rewards: one small concrete thing made strange — the light on your face — with a gap that keeps opening after the line ends.

> The light on your face tonight left its star before your grandmother was born — and the star that sent it may already be gone.

## Why it works

Three quiet facts stack inside one sentence: the light is touching you now, it set out before your grandmother was born, and the star that sent it may already be dead — so the light outlives its own source, and the gap between the words and the thing keeps widening after the line ends. Nothing is explained; every word is doing work.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` — or to `$OUTPUT_PATH` when that is set — and refuses to write anything outside the 1–200 character window the rules allow. The output is deterministic: every run writes the same bytes.
